Common questions

Where does the data come from?

From open sources including the World Bank, the United Nations, the archived CIA World Factbook, Wikidata and Natural Earth. Tap any figure to see its definition, year, source and licence. The full list is in Settings › Sources & licences.

Why do two countries show different years?

Each figure comes from a reviewed source snapshot bundled with the app. Sources update countries at different times, so years can differ. Newer source releases may exist until the next app data update. The year is always shown next to the value.

Why is a figure missing?

A figure is left out when no source with suitable terms has a recent value for that country. Missing figures are shown as missing. They are never replaced with zero or with a guess.

Are the rankings world rankings?

No. A rank compares the countries that have a value for that figure in the app. The number of countries compared is shown with every rank.

Which countries are included?

Atlas Versus includes 196 countries. Holy See is not included, because the sources do not publish enough comparable figures for it.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All data is inside the app, and the app makes no network requests. Data is refreshed through app updates.

Where are my favourites kept?

On your device only. See the privacy policy.
