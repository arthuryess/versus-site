Atlas Versus

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

In short: Atlas Versus does not collect any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests.

What the app stores, and where

Atlas Versus keeps your favourite comparisons, your recent comparisons and your settings (appearance and measurement units). They are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, that backup can include the app's data, under Apple's terms and your own backup settings.

What the app does not do

  • It has no sign-in and no account.
  • It has no developer-operated analytics or crash reporting SDK. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s terms.
  • It shows no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It makes no network requests. All country data is inside the app.

Sharing a comparison

When you choose Share image, the app creates a picture of the comparison and opens the system share sheet. The picture goes only where you choose to send or save it. Temporary copies are kept in the app's own storage and removed later.

Opening this website

The app can open the published policy in your system browser when you choose. The browser accesses this website under its own settings, and the website host may process normal web request information. This does not give the app access to your browsing history.

Permissions

  • Photos (add only) — on iPhone and iPad, only if you choose Save Image in the share sheet. The app can add the picture to your library; it cannot read your photos.

The app asks for no other permission.

Deleting your data

Remove a favourite with its heart button. Deleting the app and its data removes its local records. Offloading an iOS app retains its data; deleting a Mac app alone can leave its local storage container. Images saved or sent elsewhere remain at their chosen destination. Contact support if you need help removing local records.

Children

Atlas Versus is not directed at children, and because it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome on the Atlas Versus support page.
